# Identities

You can associate your web2 account, on-chain reputation, or domain ownership to your DEMOS address using the DEMOS sdk.

The following providers are supported:

**Web2 accounts**

1. [Twitter](/sdk/web2/identities/twitter)
2. [Github](/sdk/web2/identities/github)
3. [Discord](/sdk/web2/identities/discord)
4. [Telegram](/sdk/web2/identities/telegram)

**Reputation & Proof of Personhood**

5. [Nomis](/sdk/web2/identities/nomis) — multi-chain wallet reputation score.
6. [Ethos](/sdk/web2/identities/ethos) — peer-reviewed credibility score for EVM wallets.
7. [Human Passport](/sdk/web2/identities/human-passport) — Proof of Personhood (formerly Gitcoin Passport).

**Domains**

8. [Unstoppable Domains](/sdk/web2/identities/unstoppable-domains) — link a UD domain (EVM or Solana signing flow).
